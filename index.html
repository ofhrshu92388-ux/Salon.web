Hair Care

Traya offers a science-based hair fall solution that targets the root cause of hair loss, not just the symptoms. Its plan combines three approaches: Ayurveda, dermatology, and nutrition.

The process has three steps. First, you take a 2-4 minute online hair test to identify what’s behind your hair fall. Second, based on your answers, experts suggest a personalised plan. Third, a Traya hair coach guides you through your journey, which typically lasts 3-5 months.

The plan looks at five key factors that affect hair health: nutrition, digestion, hormones, stress, and environment. For example, nutrient deficiencies can slow growth, while high stress can disrupt the natural hair cycle and increase shedding.

Daily use is simple: the “2x2 routine” takes about two minutes, twice a day. Traya says 93% of users noticed results within five months, though this comes from its own internal study (December 2022), not independent research.

Grooming

Choosing the right haircut is essential for enhancing your appearance and highlighting your best features. Every face shape is unique, and the right hairstyle can create balance, improve proportions, and complement your personality. Before selecting a haircut, consider your face shape, hair texture, thickness, and personal style. Oval faces suit various hairstyles, while round faces benefit from cuts that add height and definition. Square faces look great with softer layers and textured styles, whereas longer faces can be balanced with fringes and fuller hairstyles.

Your lifestyle and grooming routine also play an important role in choosing the perfect haircut. A professional barber can recommend styles that suit your facial structure and preferences. Whether you prefer a modern fade, classic side part, textured crop, or longer hairstyle, choose a cut that makes you feel confident and comfortable. Communicate clearly with your barber and maintain your haircut regularly to achieve a clean, stylish, and well-groomed appearance.
